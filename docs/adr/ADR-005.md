# ADR-005: Lockout lasts for a duration rather than manual reactivation

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 08/20/2026

## Context
After 3 failed pin attempts on an approved scan, the card will be locked out and denied access. We need to find out how to reactivate the card after being locked out.

Initially, failed attempts was held with no way to reset them except for reuploading the code. Manual reactivation was being considered, but thats planned for the dashboard section which is yet to be implemented.

## Decision
A failed attempt count will belong to each individual user paired with a lockout start time. After 3 failed attempts, the card will be locked for 24 hours. After 24 hours, failed attempts resets.

## Consequences
- Positive: Avoids being locked out until device reuploads code.
- Negative: Admin cannot manually reactivate someone's card early. Must wait the full 24 hours.
